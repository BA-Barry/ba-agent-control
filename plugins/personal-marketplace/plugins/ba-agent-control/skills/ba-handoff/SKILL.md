# Prepare a handoff

Summarize the task outcome, changed paths, decisions, commands run, observed results, unresolved findings, and artifacts or receipts. Cite evidence rather than copying full logs.

State what the next role should verify first and what it must not assume. Keep pending approvals and failed gates visible. Do not mark the task complete or invent verification that did not occur.

Return a structured handoff that can survive restart or compaction.
